1. Project definition

Goal Grid is a three-month goal-execution programme for business owners, scheduled for January–March 2027. The website is a compact, single-page information and conversion experience. It is not a web application or a participant portal.





Primary objective

Help a visitor quickly understand what Goal Grid is, who it is for, what participation involves, what it costs, and how to take the next step.





Primary conversion

Every primary call to action opens WhatsApp with a prefilled enquiry message. The visitor reviews, edits if necessary, and sends the message themselves.





Scope boundaries





One responsive landing page; compact anchor navigation.





Use the existing BMB Strategy & Advisory logo supplied by the client. Do not create a separate Goal Grid logo.





Frontend only. No database, authentication, admin dashboard, waitlist database, custom API, or website form submission.





Persistent WhatsApp contact action on desktop and mobile.





All unknown programme details remain clearly marked for client confirmation.



IMPORTANT  A WhatsApp click does not automatically submit or store a visitor’s information. The visitor must send the prefilled message inside WhatsApp.

E. The GRID Method

A simple framework for moving from intention to execution:

G — Goal  Define what you want to achieve and make the target clear.

R — Roadmap  Break the goal into a practical path with priorities and milestones.

I — Implementation  Turn the roadmap into action through consistent daily execution.

D — Discipline  Build the accountability and habits needed to keep moving.

4. Design foundation

Design personality: minimalist, warm, modern, welcoming, and executive. It should feel credible and considered, not corporate-cold, generic, or visually noisy.





Brand colours



Deep Navy#142642



Gold#C9A45C



White#FFFFFF

Supporting text colour: dark charcoal (working value #30343B; confirm against the client’s official brand specification). Supporting neutral surfaces may use a very light cool grey, but navy, gold, and white remain the dominant identity.





Typography





Headings: Sora — geometric, crisp, modern; use Medium/Semibold weights and generous line spacing.





Body and interface: Manrope — highly legible, clean, and refined; use Regular/Medium.





Use a restrained type scale. Avoid overly thin weights, excessive all-caps, decorative display fonts, and too many font families.

These are proposed web fonts, not client-confirmed corporate fonts. If BMB has an official typeface, prioritize the supplied brand guideline.





Layout and visual language





Content width: approximately 1,160–1,220px; comfortable margins and generous whitespace.





Desktop section spacing: approximately 88–112px; mobile: approximately 56–72px.





Cards: use only where they clarify the GRID method, programme inclusions, or key facts; avoid a card for every paragraph.





Buttons: clear solid navy primary button with white text; gold used as a restrained accent or secondary highlight.





Use real host photography and authentic programme imagery when supplied. Do not use fabricated testimonials or generic stock imagery that implies actual participants.





Use a restrained graphic language inspired by alignment, pathways, milestones, and structured progress — without creating a new logo.





Motion and interaction





Subtle fade/slide reveals as sections enter view; reveal once, not continuously.





Small hover/focus transitions on buttons and navigation; approximately 200–300ms.





A restrained visual treatment for the four GRID stages; no complex animated dashboard or heavy parallax.





Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep mobile animations lightweight.
